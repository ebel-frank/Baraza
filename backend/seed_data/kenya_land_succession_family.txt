## Land Act, Section 6: Unregistered Land and Customary Rights
Customary land rights, including those held under community tenure, are recognized as valid
interests in land even where no formal title has been issued. A dispute over the boundary of
unregistered community land may be handled through community dispute resolution, but any
dispute over land already registered under a title deed falls outside this mechanism.

## Law of Succession Act, Section 32: Exemption for Agricultural Land
Certain succession disputes concerning agricultural land in specified areas may be subject to
customary law and can be addressed through community elders' councils. Where a will exists and
is contested as fraudulent, or where a beneficiary alleges they were unlawfully excluded, the
matter should be referred to the Magistrate's Court (Succession Cause).

## Law of Succession Act, Section 40: Polygamous Households
Where a deceased person is survived by more than one spouse, distribution of the estate follows
a specific statutory formula rather than informal agreement alone. Community mediators may help
the family reach a shared understanding of the process but should not purport to issue a final,
binding division of a polygamous estate.

## Matrimonial Property Act, Section 7: Contribution and Ownership
Property acquired during marriage is presumed to belong to the spouses in proportion to their
respective contributions, whether monetary or non-monetary (including domestic and farm labor).
Disputes over division of matrimonial property upon separation may be mediated, but allegations
of domestic violence connected to the dispute should be referred to the appropriate authorities
rather than resolved solely through informal mediation.
